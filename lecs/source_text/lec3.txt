Why would we want stochastic/SDE dynamics?
In theory: All diffusion coefficients lead to the same result (sample from data
distribution).
In practice:
 -   Training error: Neural network has not perfectly learnt the marginal vector
     field/score.
 -   Simulation error: We need to simulate SDE/ODE leading to discretization
     error.
Downstream applications: Fine-tuning, inference-time optimization, etc. might
require stochastic evolution


Good news: ODE sampling often leads to the best results.
Therefore, SDE sampling is an option, not a must!
